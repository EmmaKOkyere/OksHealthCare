How OksHealthCare Works

You can request healthcare support even when mobile data or Wi-Fi is unavailable.

🌐

Online

Use OksHealthCare normally when you have an internet connection.

✉️

Offline SMS

Without internet, you can send an SMS callback request using your mobile network.

📞

Phone Call

You can also call the OksHealthCare medical support line.

Medical Emergency?

OksHealthCare's callback system should not replace emergency medical services. If someone's life may be in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service or seek emergency medical care.
